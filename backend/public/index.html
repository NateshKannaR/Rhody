How to Install on Your Android Phone

1 Download the APK

Tap the green "Download APK" button above directly on your Android phone's browser or transfer the 57MB file from your PC.

2 Allow & Install

Open the downloaded Rhody-v1.0.0.apk file. If prompted by Android, enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser and tap Install.

3 Mount & Ride

Launch Rhody, mount your phone securely to your handlebars, calibrate level, and start recording precision telemetry!
